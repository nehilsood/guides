What each chapter contains, and how to work it

Every chapter has the same parts, meant to be used in this order:

A jargon-free primer, so no chapter opens on vocabulary you would have to look up. The résumé line itself, quoted, so the chapter and the page can never drift apart. Four question banks — about seven toolbox, ten fundamentals, twelve deep-dive, eight edge-and-exception — each with a written answer and a red flag naming the response that ends the loop. One diagram, twelve marks maximum, that you cover and redraw in sixty seconds. A spoken script of about 75 seconds in four beats, where the fourth beat is always what the decision cost. A STAR story for the behavioural round.

The spoken scripts are the half people skip and the half the interview actually tests. Say them out loud. Reading them silently feels like learning and mostly is not.

How the spacing works

Opening a chapter records a visit; answering its quizzes records how you did. This page then tells you what is due for review on an expanding schedule — 1, 3, 7, 21, 60 days — pushed further out when you answer cleanly first time and pulled back in when you do not. It is stored in this browser only, so it will not follow you to another machine, and clearing site data resets it.
