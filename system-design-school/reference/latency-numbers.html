The one comparison that matters most

A datacenter round trip is roughly 5,000× a memory reference. A cross-continent round trip is roughly 2,000,000×. This is why a cache hit and a cache miss are different kinds of event rather than different speeds, and why moving a hop off the critical path beats optimising it.

Percentile arithmetic

QuestionAnswer
n sequential hops, each with p99 = T. Turn p99? Magnitude stays near T; probability of hitting a tail becomes 1 − 0.99ⁿ. Five hops → ~5% of turns. Percentiles are not additive.
Parallel hops? Cost is the maximum, not the sum. But the tail is worse than any single hop's: you wait for the slowest of n draws.
How many samples to trust a p99? At least 1,000 for a stable p99; 10,000+ for p99.9. Below that you are reporting noise with a decimal point.
Which percentile definition? Nearest-rank: ceil(q/100 × N) − 1. Never interpolate — it invents a value nobody measured, in the only region anyone argues about.
Closed-loop generator, C workers, stall of duration S. Bad samples? C. Independent of S. This is coordinated omission, and it is why the reported tail tracks your worker count rather than your service.
Open-loop generator, rate R, stall of duration S? R × S affected requests — which is what a real user population would have experienced.

Budget rules of thumb

  • Never let the median approach the ceiling. A median at the ceiling means roughly half of requests breach. Aim for the median at 25–35% of budget.
  • Budget against p99, not p50. The ceiling is a promise about the worst acceptable case, so compare it to a tail statistic.
  • Reserve for retries explicitly. A hop with one retry can consume double its budget. If the budget has no room for that, the retry policy is decorative.
  • Attack the largest hop first, and only it. A 42% hop halved beats a 6% hop eliminated.
  • Move hops off the critical path before optimising them. A hop that does not need the previous hop's output should not be waiting for it.
  • Separate floors from choices. TLS handshakes, silence windows and speed-of-light are floors. Label them once so nobody spends a sprint on them.
